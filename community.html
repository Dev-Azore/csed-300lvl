What we do often

Good habits make work easy.

We have a few regular things we do together to stay on track and keep learning.

🗣️
Weekly
TechTalk (30 min)

One student teaches something new to everyone. We take turns every week. No pressure, just sharing.

🔎
Every 2 Weeks
Help Each Other

We look at each other's code and designs. We give kind and helpful advice to make the work better.

🚀
Every 2 Weeks
Show and Tell

Each team shows their progress to the group. It helps us feel proud and stay on schedule.

Who leads us

The leadership team.

We have roles that change every semester so everyone gets a chance to lead.

👑
Group Leader

Helps the whole group stay focused and makes final decisions. This role rotates to a new student later.

🌿
Track Leads

The experts for each skill path (Engineering, Data, Design, DevOps). They help the seniors mentor the juniors.

📅
Events Planner

Plans our big show-and-tell days and invites guest speakers from the industry.

💬
Community Friend

Makes sure new members feel welcome and checks that everyone is happy and healthy.

📚
Knowledge Keeper

Records our lessons and keeps our online library organized for everyone to use.

Other useful skills

More than just tech.

We also learn how to be good teammates and leaders.

🎤
Speaking Up

Practice talking in front of others so you feel brave during presentations.

🤝
Solving Group Problems

Learn how to deal with disagreements calmly and stay friends with your team.

✍️
Writing Clearly

Learn how to write notes and posts that people can understand easily.
