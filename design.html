Product & Design Path

The Beauty Track.

In this track, we learn how to make tools that people love to use. We think about the user first and build a beautiful experience.

Track Leader

Muttaka Muhammad

UI/UX Designer

Muttaka Muhammad is a designer who makes apps easy and fun to use. He helps students learn how to think about the people who use our tools. He believes that good design is simple and clear for everyone.

What you can learn:

🎨
UI Design

Making the website look brand new and professional. You will learn about colors, fonts, and how to make things look beautiful.

👥
UX Research

Talking to real people to find out what they need. You will learn how to ask the right questions to build better tools.

📈
Product Management

Planning the whole project and making sure we build it on time. You will learn how to lead a team and keep everyone focused.
