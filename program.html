The Road Map

Five steps to success.

We grow together. First we build trust, then we build projects, and then we teach others.

1
Weeks 1–2 Getting Started

The Foundation

We get to know each other and set our goals.

🏛️
Group Setup
  • Pick our group name and rules
  • Meet our mentors and leaders
  • Find a "buddy" to help you along
🔍
Skill Check
  • See what everyone is good at
  • Pick your main path of learning
  • Get matched with a mentor
2
Weeks 3–4 Finding Problems

Project Discovery

We find out what real problems people have.

🧭
Research
  • Talk to other students about their needs
  • Pick the top 3 problems to solve
  • Form small teams of 3–4 people
📋
Planning
  • Decide what tools to use
  • Make a plan for the next 4 weeks
  • Set up where our code will live
3
Weeks 5–8 Building Time

The Build

This is where we build the real project.

⚡
Work Cycles
  • Work in quick 2-week steps
  • Short daily meetings to stay on track
  • Share what we learned each week
🎙️
Soft Skills
  • Learn how to speak in public
  • Learn how to work under pressure
  • Learn how to solve team problems
4
Week 9 Helping Others

Scale & Teach

We share our knowledge with the whole class.

📚
Teaching
  • Teach one class session to everyone
  • Record our lessons for the future
  • Invite real users to try our projects
🔄
New Leaders
  • Vote for new student leaders
  • Help the next group get ready
  • Look back at what we achieved
5
Week 10 The Finale

Demo & Celebration

We show what we built and celebrate our growth.

🏆
Demo Day
  • Show our projects to teachers and experts
  • Finish our personal portfolios
  • Exchange notes on LinkedIn
🎊
Legacy
  • Hand over our lessons to the next group
  • Recognition awards for everyone
  • Celebrate 10 weeks of hard work!

Finish what you start.

10 weeks. One community. A legacy that lasts.
